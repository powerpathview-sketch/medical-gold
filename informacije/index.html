Indikacije za primenu HBOT-a

Hiperbarična kiseonična terapija primenjuje se kod stanja za koja je takav tretman preporučen od strane Američkog udruženja za podvodnu i hiperbaričnu medicinu (UHMS) i Evropskog komiteta za hiperbaričnu medicinu (ECHM).

Hitna stanja

  • Arterijska gasna embolija
  • Bolesti ronjenja (dekompresiona bolest, barotraumatska gasna embolija)
  • Opekotine
  • Trovanje ugljen-monoksidom (CO), udisanje dima i para
  • Nekrotizirajuće infekcije mekog tkiva i gasna gangrena
  • Akutna gluvoća i/ili tinitus
  • Akutno slepilo

U hirurgiji

  • Termičke povrede (opekotine, hipotermija i promrzline)
  • Povrede od gnječenja i eksplozije
  • Nekrotizirajuća upala mekih delova tela, akutni nekrotizirajući fasciitis
  • Gasna gangrena (klostridijalna mionekroza)
  • Akutni i hronični osteomijelitis
  • Aseptična nekroza kuka
  • Nepravilno zarasli prelom kosti
  • Transplantirani kožni režnjevi (Mb. Buerger, Sy. i Mb. Raynaud, Mb. Sudeck)
  • Subakutna i hronična arterijska insuficijencija različitih uzroka
  • Hronična refraktorna upala
  • Oštećenje tkiva nakon zračenja
  • Teške povrede (traumatska ishemija), masivno krvarenje
  • Rane koje sporo zarastaju
  • Periferni vaskularni ulkusi (arterijski, venski, dekubitalni, dijabetički, trofički, neuropatski)
  • Sportske povrede

U neurologiji i neurohirurgiji

  • Vrtoglavica, glavobolja, migrena
  • Multipla skleroza
  • Cerebralna paraliza i traumatske povrede mozga
  • Apscesi mozga
  • Ograničeni CVI, moždani udar i povrede mozga i kičmene moždine
  • Cerebralni edem (toksična encefalopatija, vazogeni, traumatski)
  • Postanoksična encefalopatija
  • Rani organski sindrom mozga (bolest malih krvnih sudova)
  • Autizam, lajmska bolest
  • Trigeminalna neuralgija
  • Akutna pareza facijalnog nerva (Bellova paraliza)
  • Vestibularni poremećaji
  • Iznenadna gluvoća i/ili tinitus
  • Akutno slepilo, okluzija centralne arterije retine i tromboza centralne vene retine
  • Dijabetička retinopatija
  • Neuritis optičkog nerva
  • Glaukom otvorenog ugla

U internoj medicini

  • Kronova bolest, ulcerozni kolitis, pseudomembranozni kolitis
  • Akutni reumatoidni artritis
  • Peptički ulkus želuca i pilorusa
  • Dijabetes sa komplikacijama — hronične komplikacije najčešće se javljaju na očima, bubrezima i nogama, otprilike 2–10 godina nakon početka dijabetesa: dijabetička nefropatija, dijabetička retinopatija, dijabetička neuropatija, dijabetičko stopalo, mikroangiopatija, dijabetička gangrena
  • Kardiovaskularne bolesti: nakon akutnog infarkta miokarda, ateroskleroza, hronična srčana insuficijencija
  • Priprema pre hirurških intervencija u kardiovaskularnoj hirurgiji

Indikacije koje još nisu potvrđene u velikom broju slučajeva

  • Muški sterilitet
  • Impotencija vaskularnog uzroka

Dodatne indikacije

  • Tzv. menadžerske bolesti
  • Anti-aging program (protiv starenja)
  • Gubitak težine

Indications for HBOT

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy is applied for conditions where such treatment is recommended by the Undersea and Hyperbaric Medical Society (UHMS) and the European Committee for Hyperbaric Medicine (ECHM).

Emergency conditions

  • Arterial gas embolism
  • Diving disorders (decompression sickness, barotraumatic gas embolism)
  • Burns
  • Carbon monoxide (CO) poisoning, smoke and fume inhalation
  • Necrotizing soft tissue infections and gas gangrene
  • Acute hearing loss and/or tinnitus
  • Acute vision loss

In surgery

  • Thermal injuries (burns, hypothermia and frostbite)
  • Crush and blast injuries
  • Necrotizing soft tissue inflammation, acute necrotizing fasciitis
  • Gas gangrene (clostridial myonecrosis)
  • Acute and chronic osteomyelitis
  • Aseptic necrosis of the hip
  • Improperly healed bone fractures
  • Skin grafts and flaps (Buerger's disease, Raynaud's syndrome and disease, Sudeck's atrophy)
  • Subacute and chronic arterial insufficiency of various causes
  • Chronic refractory inflammation
  • Radiation tissue damage
  • Severe injuries (traumatic ischemia), massive bleeding
  • Slow-healing wounds
  • Peripheral vascular ulcers (arterial, venous, pressure, diabetic, trophic, neuropathic)
  • Sports injuries

In neurology and neurosurgery

  • Vertigo, headache, migraine
  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Cerebral palsy and traumatic brain injuries
  • Brain abscesses
  • Limited stroke, cerebrovascular insult and injuries of the brain and spinal cord
  • Cerebral edema (toxic encephalopathy, vasogenic, traumatic)
  • Post-anoxic encephalopathy
  • Early organic brain syndrome (small vessel disease)
  • Autism, Lyme disease
  • Trigeminal neuralgia
  • Acute facial nerve paresis (Bell's palsy)
  • Vestibular disorders
  • Sudden hearing loss and/or tinnitus
  • Acute vision loss, central retinal artery occlusion and central retinal vein thrombosis
  • Diabetic retinopathy
  • Optic neuritis
  • Open-angle glaucoma

In internal medicine

  • Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, pseudomembranous colitis
  • Acute rheumatoid arthritis
  • Peptic ulcer of the stomach and pylorus
  • Diabetes with complications — chronic complications most often affect the eyes, kidneys and legs, appearing roughly 2–10 years after diabetes onset: diabetic nephropathy, diabetic retinopathy, diabetic neuropathy, diabetic foot, microangiopathy, diabetic gangrene
  • Cardiovascular disease: after acute myocardial infarction, atherosclerosis, chronic heart failure
  • Preparation before cardiovascular surgery

Indications not yet confirmed in a large number of cases

  • Male infertility
  • Impotence of vascular origin

Additional indications

  • So-called managerial (stress-related) illnesses
  • Anti-aging program
  • Weight loss
