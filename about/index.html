About Us

Medical Gold Ltd. — Belgrade, Serbia

Ko smo mi

Medical Gold d.o.o. je kompanija sa sedištem u Beogradu, specijalizovana za prodaju hiperbaričnih komora i medicinske opreme na teritoriji Republike Srbije.

Kompanija je registrovana u Agenciji za privredne registre (PIB: 115740758, matični broj: 22310135), sa sedištem na adresi Bulevar Mihajla Pupina 153, 11070 Beograd (Novi Beograd). Direktor kompanije je Vladana Jovanović.

Naša misija je da klinikama, wellness centrima i zdravstvenim ustanovama u Srbiji obezbedimo pristup najsavremenijoj medicinskoj tehnologiji za hiperbaričnu kiseoničnu terapiju (HBO).

Saradnja sa OxyMed Poliklinikom

Medical Gold posluje u saradnji sa OxyMed Poliklinikom d.o.o. iz Kopra, Slovenija. OxyMed je specijalizovana klinika za hiperbaričnu medicinu i otorinolaringologiju, sa dozvolom Ministarstva zdravlja Republike Slovenije.

Mr. sci. med. Vladan Stanojković, dr. med.

Na čelu OxyMed Poliklinike je mr. sci. med. Vladan Stanojković — specijalista otorinolaringologije koji se hiperbaričnom medicinom bavi od 2011. godine. Dr Stanojković ima dugogodišnje praktično iskustvo u primeni hiperbaričnog kiseonika (HBO terapija).

Zahvaljujući ovoj saradnji, Medical Gold svojim klijentima u Srbiji obezbeđuje:

  • Pristup proverenim hiperbaričnim komorama
  • Stručnu podršku i konsultacije
  • Obuku za rukovanje opremom
  • Servis i održavanje uređaja
  • Sertifikate i dokumentaciju potrebnu za zdravstvene ustanove

Naša dokumentacija

Za svaki uređaj obezbeđujemo prateću dokumentaciju i sertifikate proizvođača — slobodno ih zatražite pre kupovine. Registraciju medicinskih sredstava sprovodimo u skladu sa propisima Republike Srbije.

Who we are

Medical Gold Ltd. is a Belgrade-based company specializing in the distribution of hyperbaric chambers and medical equipment across the Republic of Serbia.

The company is registered with the Serbian Business Registers Agency (Tax ID: 115740758, registration number: 22310135), with its registered office at Bulevar Mihajla Pupina 153, 11070 Belgrade (Novi Beograd). The company director is Vladana Jovanović.

Our mission is to provide clinics, wellness centers, and healthcare facilities in Serbia with access to state-of-the-art hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBO) technology.

Partnership with OxyMed Polyclinic

Medical Gold operates in partnership with OxyMed Polyclinic d.o.o. based in Koper, Slovenia. OxyMed is a specialized clinic for hyperbaric medicine and otolaryngology, authorized by the Slovenian Ministry of Health.

Vladan Stanojković, MSc, MD

OxyMed Polyclinic is led by Vladan Stanojković, MSc, MD — an ENT specialist who has worked in hyperbaric medicine since 2011. Dr. Stanojković has years of hands-on experience with hyperbaric oxygen (HBO) therapy.

Through this partnership, Medical Gold provides its clients in Serbia with:

  • Access to proven hyperbaric chambers
  • Expert support and consultation
  • Equipment operation training
  • Service and maintenance
  • Certificates and documentation required for healthcare facilities

Our Documentation

Every device we sell comes with manufacturer documentation and certificates — feel free to ask for them before buying. Medical device registration is handled in line with Serbian regulations.
